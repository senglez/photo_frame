FAQ

Questions buyers ask before the first order

Something missing? Ask in the inquiry form and we answer within 24 hours.

What is your minimum order quantity?

300 to 500 pieces per design for our existing models, and 500 to 1,000 pieces for custom sizes or colors. Mixed designs can be combined in one shipment.

How fast can I get a sample?

Standard samples ship in 7 days and samples with custom packaging in 10 days, by express courier. A small sample fee applies per design and is credited against your bulk order. Two minor revisions are free.

What is the lead time for bulk orders?

14 days for standard models and 24 days for fully custom orders, counted from sample approval and deposit. Ocean freight to the US West Coast takes about 14 to 20 days.

Which payment terms do you accept?

T/T with 30% deposit and 70% before shipment. PayPal for samples, and Alibaba Trade Assurance on request. After three to five repeat orders we can discuss L/C at sight or longer payment windows.

Do you quote FOB, CIF or DDP?

We quote FOB Ningbo by default. CIF and DDP are available on request, and DDP is often the simplest choice for first time importers.

Can you make custom sizes, finishes and packaging?

Yes. Custom sizes usually add USD 0.10 to 0.50 per piece, and special finishes such as gilding, distressed or textured add USD 0.30 to 1.50 per piece. Non standard sizes may need a one time tooling fee, which we waive for orders above 5,000 pieces. Tooling you pay for belongs to you.

How do you protect my designs?

We sign an NDA before you share designs. Your packaging, specs and tooling are marked exclusive to your account and never offered to other buyers.

How do you control quality?

We inspect materials on arrival, check during production and run a final random inspection against your approved sample before packing. Our measured defect rate is under 1.5%, and we replace or credit anything above that. Third party inspection by SGS, BV or your own QC is welcome.

Which compliance documents can you provide?

FSC claim labels and transaction certificates for wood and MDF, our current BSCI audit report, and ISPM 15 treated wooden packaging. We can arrange CPSIA testing for children's products sold in the US.

Can I see the factory before ordering?

Yes. Book a live video tour of our 20,000 sqm factory, or visit us in Jinhua. Third party audits are welcome.
